# Reward Priority Protocol (why the hash chain exists)

The hash-chained ledger (coldcase_master.EvidenceLog) + external timestamp
anchoring (export_anchor_manifest) establishes PRIORITY OF DISCOVERY for
reward claims without public disclosure. Do it in this order.

## 1. Read the reward terms FIRST (they vary and they bite)
- Most rewards pay only for information given DIRECTLY to the investigating
  agency (or its designated tip line) that LEADS TO the recovery - a hash in
  your repo earns nothing by itself; the TIP does. The ledger is your proof
  if priority is later disputed.
- Common exclusions: law enforcement, family members, anyone with inside
  knowledge of the crime, persons who participated. Some rewards require the
  claimant NOT be anonymous at payment time.
- Expiry dates exist. Record the reward's posted terms (screenshot -> hash it
  into the ledger too).

## 2. The sequence that protects both the reward and the patent
  a. Run the case -> candidates + evidence log entries accumulate.
  b. BEFORE submitting any tip: export_anchor_manifest() + anchor the manifest
     (OpenTimestamps is free and creates no prior-art exposure - hash-only).
  c. Submit the tip through the official channel only. Log the submission
     (date, channel, reference number) as a ledger entry.
  d. If recovery follows and priority is disputed: the anchored manifest +
     verify_chain() output is your evidence package.
  e. NEVER publish the manifest or predictions publicly before filing the
     patent - anchoring proves existence without disclosure; publishing
     sacrifices novelty.

## 3. Active cases (any active case or one with an open reward)
Tips go to the investigating agency and nowhere else. No social media, no
podcasts, no "we predicted this" posts until the agency acts and the patent
is filed - premature publicity can void the reward, compromise the case,
and (pre-filing) destroy foreign patent rights.

## 4. If a search ever recovers physical evidence
Do not touch or move it. Photograph in place, record GPS, notify LE
immediately. The ledger supplements official chain of custody - it does not
replace it, and reward terms universally route through LE.

## 5. Cadence
Anchor at every case close, before every tip, and before filing anything.
